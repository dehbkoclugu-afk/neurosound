## Description
852Hz is an offline sound app for sleep, focus, meditation, and relaxation routines.

Start from what you need right now — Sleep, Focus, Relax, or Meditate — and a matching sound begins with a timer already set. Or browse the full library: binaural beats, Solfeggio tones, and ambient sounds like rain, ocean, forest, fire, and fan.

• Ambient sounds: rain, thunderstorm, ocean, forest, stream, wind, fireplace, fan, airplane, train, and white, pink, and brown noise
• Binaural beats and single-frequency tones
• Mixer: layer up to four sounds and save your mixes
• Sleep timer that fades the sound out gently
• Lock-screen playback controls
• Favourites and saved mixes
• 21 languages, light and dark themes
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Works fully offline

Binaural beats and low-frequency tones need headphones; a phone speaker cannot reproduce them.

852Hz Premium unlocks the full library of binaural beats and Solfeggio tones and lets you use them in the mixer. Premium is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings.

852Hz is not a medical product and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Effects of sound are personal. Keep the volume at a comfortable level.

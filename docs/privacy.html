Son güncelleme: 4 Kasım 2026

NeuroSound hiçbir kişisel veri toplamaz, paylaşmaz veya geliştiriciye aktarmaz. Favoriler, kayıtlı karışımlar ve ayarlar yalnızca cihazda tutulur. Android bulut yedeklemesi kapalıdır. iPhone’da bu veriler, açıksa kendi iCloud yedeğinize dahil olabilir; bize ulaşmaz.

NeuroSound Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

NeuroSound does not collect, share, or transfer personal data to the developer. Favourites, saved mixes, and settings remain on the device. Android cloud backup is disabled. On iPhone, this data may be included in your own iCloud backup if you have it turned on; it never reaches us.

The only network access is the store’s billing service (Google Play or the Apple App Store), used when you open the Premium screen, buy, or restore a purchase. Payment details are handled by the store and are never passed to us.

The app has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking identifiers. It works offline and does not request microphone, location, or contacts access. Local data is removed when the app is uninstalled or reset in Settings.

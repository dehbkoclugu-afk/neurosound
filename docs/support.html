Uyku zamanlayıcısı nasıl çalışır? Süreyi seçin; süre dolarken ses yavaşça kısılır ve durur. Telefonu kilitleyebilirsiniz.

FAQ

I can't hear the binaural beats. Binaural beats send a different frequency to each ear, so they need headphones. A phone speaker cannot reproduce low frequencies.

How does the sleep timer work? Pick a duration; as it ends, the sound fades out and stops. You can lock your phone.

Restoring Premium. Tap “Restore purchases” on the Premium screen while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Cancelling a subscription. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Where is my data? Nothing is collected. Details: Privacy Policy.
